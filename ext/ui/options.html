Appearance

Theme and the accent gradient behind the panel.

Quiet

How long a snooze lasts by default, and a daily window that's always quiet.

Shortcuts

Open the panel or toggle mute without a click.

What's being observed

Exactly what this build currently stores and reads on this device. Nothing here is ever sent anywhere without a backend configured.

Account (testing)

Paste the access token for your test server. It stays on this device and is sent only to the server this build was configured for.

AI processing

When you add an event from selected text or an image, that content is sent to Anthropic (the maker of Claude) to read it. Your name, email and account details are not sent. This is off until you turn it on, and turning it off takes effect on the next request.

Open and close apps (optional)

Lets you type "open Zoom" or "close Slack" in the panel. It needs a small helper program installed on your computer (macOS only for now), it is off until you turn it on, and it can only touch the apps you tick below. Closing asks the app to quit, so it can still ask you to save; it never force-quits. Your browser, Finder and system apps can be opened but never closed from here. The list of your apps stays on this computer.

Delete my data

Permanently removes your settings, calendar connections, learned patterns and proposed events from the server, and clears this device. This can't be undone.

Connections

What each provider can read and write, before you connect it.
